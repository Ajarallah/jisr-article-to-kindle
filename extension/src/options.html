Article to Kindle — Settings

1. Your Kindle email

The address that ends in @kindle.com. Find it in Amazon → Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings.

2. Delivery service

The address of your sending service (the small backend that emails the EPUB to your Kindle). Default is a local server on your machine.

3. Approve the sender (required once)

Amazon silently drops any document from an address you have not approved. In the same Personal Document Settings page, under Approved Personal Document E-mail List, add the address your delivery service sends from. If you do not do this, sends will look successful but nothing arrives.
